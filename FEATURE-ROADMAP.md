# GYMFLOW feature roadmap

## Platform admin
- Gym onboarding, verification and suspension
- Subscription plans, billing and platform analytics
- Platform support and audit log

## Gym owner / staff
- Gym profile, branches, opening hours and services
- Staff roles: owner, manager, reception, trainer, billing
- Member CRM, leads and trial follow-up
- Membership plans, renewals, pause/freeze and discounts
- QR/manual attendance with audit history
- Classes, capacity, bookings and personal training
- Trainers, schedules and assignments
- Invoices, receipts, payment reconciliation and expenses
- Inventory, lockers and equipment maintenance
- Reports, exports, announcements and support

## Member portal
- Profile, member ID and QR pass
- Membership status, renewal and payment history
- Class/PT bookings and cancellations
- Trainer-approved workout plan
- Invoices, receipts and announcements
- Communication preferences and opt-out

## Automation
- Invoice PDF generation
- WhatsApp and email invoice delivery
- Configurable renewal and overdue reminders
- Class and PT reminders
- Delivery logs, retries and duplicate prevention
- Explicit consent and opt-out handling

## Delivery phases
1. Authentication + tenant-safe database + gym onboarding
2. Members + plans + memberships + attendance
3. Invoices + payment verification + receipts
4. Member portal + classes + trainers
5. WhatsApp/email automations + reporting
6. Security review, testing and production launch
